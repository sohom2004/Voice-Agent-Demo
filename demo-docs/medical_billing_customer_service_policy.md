# Medical Billing Customer Service Policy (Demo)

This document is a fictional demo policy for training and product demonstrations.
It is not legal, clinical, or regulatory guidance.

## Purpose

Customer service agents support callers with billing accounts, invoices, insurance
claims, payments, and support tickets.

## Core Service Standards

1. Verify the caller using account number, full name, and a recent invoice or claim number.
2. Use live account data for balances, claim statuses, invoices, and payments.
3. Use published policy documents for process and escalation questions.
4. Never invent claim numbers, balances, denial reasons, or ticket statuses.
5. Keep explanations concise and plain-language.

## When To Open A Ticket

Open a support ticket when:

- a claim denial needs investigation
- a payment failed and needs follow-up
- an invoice balance is disputed
- the caller requests written tracking of an issue
- the issue cannot be resolved in the current call

## Priority Guidance

- low: general questions with no financial urgency
- medium: standard billing or payment follow-ups
- high: denied claims, failed payments tied to due dates, or repeated failed contacts
- urgent: escalation cases, potential collections impact, or unresolved appeals past SLA

## Communication Rules

- Confirm the action before creating or updating a ticket.
- Provide the ticket number after successful creation.
- Offer one clear next step when information is missing.
