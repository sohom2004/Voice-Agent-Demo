# Insurance Processing Guidelines (Demo)

Fictional operational guidelines for demo conversations about insurance claim processing.

## Claim Lifecycle

Typical statuses:

- submitted
- processing
- approved
- partially_paid
- denied
- appealed
- closed

## Explaining Claim Amounts

When discussing a claim, clearly separate:

1. billed amount
2. allowed amount
3. insurance paid
4. customer responsibility
5. claim status
6. denial reason, if present

## Processing Expectations

- Newly submitted claims may remain in processing for several business days.
- Partially paid claims usually create customer responsibility that appears on an invoice.
- Approved claims may still leave a deductible or coinsurance balance.
- Appealed claims should not be described as approved until the live status changes.

## Agent Behavior

- Use database tools for live claim and billing-account data.
- Use policy documents only for process questions.
- If a claim cannot be found, say so and ask for the claim number or account number.
