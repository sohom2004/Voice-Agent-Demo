# Claim Denial And Appeal Policy (Demo)

Fictional demo policy for medical billing BPO voice-agent demonstrations.
Not real payer policy and not legal advice.

## Denied Claims

A claim may be denied for reasons such as:

- missing prior authorization
- medical-necessity review
- coding inconsistency
- eligibility or coverage limits
- duplicate submission

Agents must read the denial reason from the live claim record and must not invent one.

## When To Escalate A Denied Claim

Escalate when any of the following are true:

1. Prior authorization evidence exists but the claim remains denied.
2. The denial affects an overdue balance greater than $250.
3. The appeal window is within 10 days of expiration.
4. The caller has already submitted supporting documents and no update is available.
5. The claim status is appealed and stalled beyond 14 days.

## Information Required For An Appeal

Collect and document:

- claim number
- service date
- provider name
- procedure code
- denial reason
- supporting authorization or clinical notes reference
- preferred contact method for the follow-up

## Appeal Handling Steps

1. Confirm the claim status and denial reason from live records.
2. Summarize the appeal request to the caller.
3. Create or update a ticket only after explicit confirmation.
4. Route high-priority denial tickets to Claims Review.
5. Route urgent stalled appeals to Senior Claims Escalations.
