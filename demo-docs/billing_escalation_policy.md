# Billing Escalation Policy (Demo)

Fictional escalation policy used only for product demos.

## Escalation Triggers

Escalate a billing issue when:

- a ticket remains unresolved after two customer contacts
- an overdue invoice exceeds $400 and payment attempts have failed
- a claim appeal is urgent or stalled
- the account status is collections_hold
- the caller reports financial hardship tied to an incorrect balance

## Ticket Escalation Rules

1. Set priority to urgent for stalled appeals and collections risk.
2. Assign Senior Claims Escalations for claim-driven urgencies.
3. Assign Payments Team for failed or missing payment applications.
4. Add a clear comment describing what changed and why escalation was needed.
5. Never escalate silently — confirm the update with the caller first.

## High Priority Billing Issues

Treat as high priority when:

- a denied claim creates immediate balance impact
- a payment failed on an already overdue invoice
- the caller cannot access care documentation needed for appeal
- the due date is within 5 days and balance remains unpaid

## After Escalation

Provide:

- ticket number
- assigned team
- current status
- expected follow-up path in plain language
